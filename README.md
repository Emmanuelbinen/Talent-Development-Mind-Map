# Leadership Development / Talent Development Mind Map

An interactive, single-file mind map exploring **leadership development** and **talent development**, organized into three main branches.

**Live demo:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/leadership-talent-development-mind-map/

---

## Overview

The map starts from a central node, *Leadership Development / Talent Development*, and expands into three main branches:

| Branch | Focus | Key sub-branches |
|---|---|---|
| **Talent Development / Talent Management** | Approaches to developing people and performance | Individualist Approach, Systems Approach, Modern Approach, Multigenerational, Talent Management |
| **Staff Retention** | Keeping and growing talent | Work Environment, Retention Strategies |
| **Knowledge Management** | Systems, structures, and sector application | Knowledge, Human Resources, Research, Performance Management, Industries |

The **Industries** sub-branch covers integration, healthcare systems, tech providers, healthcare providers, and innovation.

## Features

- Collapse or expand any branch by clicking its circle
- Expand all or collapse all with one click
- Zoom in and out
- Drag or scroll to pan around the map
- Light and dark mode, following your device setting
